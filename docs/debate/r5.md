# Round 5 brief: the one addition that makes Drape a winner

Read `CLAUDE.md` (hackathon rules, judging), `docs/product.md` (what is built), `README.md`, and `docs/submission/devpost.md`
(the current pitch). The product is live: https://noor-modest.ramy-comm.workers.dev (demo store with the button) and
https://drape.ramy-comm.workers.dev/app/ (buyer app, seller console at `#/seller`).

## Where Drape is today (built, tested, deployed)

- A store embeds one script tag and a "Try on with Drape" button. Buyer: one face photo → YouCam Facial Color Tones → palette
  (undertone, contrast, colours that lift / fight, lip shade, metal) → catalogue re-sorted with verdicts → Compare: same garment in
  the fighting colour beside the lifting colour on the buyer's own body (Clothes VTO) → lip shade (Makeup VTO) and earrings in the
  palette metal (Earrings VTO) → the bundle goes back to the store's own cart.
- Seller console: products, colourways (colour picker), listing links, unit ledger, a view-only judge key.
- URL-only Chrome extension: on a product page whose link a seller registered, it opens Drape for that product (no crawling;
  owner rule: official doors only).
- English and Arabic RTL, responsive phone to laptop, 24h photo deletion, unit ledger with caps, replay fallback.

## The question

The owner wants ONE more thing, "something special", that:
1. moves Drape from a strong entry to the **winner** under the four equal criteria (technical implementation of the YouCam APIs,
   design, potential impact, quality of the idea), and
2. makes **stores want to add Drape** (the business pull: why would a merchant rush to install it?).

## Hard constraints

- Deadline **Nov 2, 2026, 11:45 AM ET**; it is Oct 12. One developer with an AI pair. Video, screenshots and the write-up still
  have to be made, so the addition must be buildable in **about 3–5 days** and demo in **≤ 25 seconds** of a 3-minute video.
- YouCam units: about 950 left; 400 are reserved for judges (live budget). Every paid call needs the owner's approval. Name the
  API(s) and the unit cost per buyer and per store.
- Must not break what works; replay mode (0 units) must still cover the judge path.
- Owner rules: no crawling or reading other sites' pages; synthetic or owned imagery only; no third-party trademarks in the video.
- It must use the YouCam APIs more deeply or more cleverly (the first tie-breaker is technical implementation), not add a
  chatbot or a generic "AI stylist" layer.

## Answer format (≤ 700 words)

1. **The one addition** (name it, one paragraph): what the buyer and the store see.
2. **Why it wins**: map it to each of the four judging criteria, and say why a judge remembers it after 40 entries.
3. **Why stores run to install it**: the merchant's money reason, with a number you can defend or clearly label as an assumption.
4. **YouCam APIs and units**: which calls, how many units per buyer / per store, and how replay covers it.
5. **Build plan**: the concrete changes (files, endpoints, screens) in days, plus what you would cut if it slips.
6. **The 25-second video beat.**
7. **Biggest risk** and how you would know in day 1 whether it works.
8. Name one alternative you considered and rejected, and why.
