# Round 4: the product registry as the business

Context: read product.md (the current "Drape Network" product, gate test passed: flat-lay, on-model, real colourway pair keeps shape, makeup on selfie).

## The owner's idea
When a buyer is on Amazon (or any store) and clicks the Drape extension, Drape first asks: **is this exact product already on Drape and
linked to this store listing?** Sellers add their products to Drape (clean colourway photos, category, colours) and attach the product's
links on other stores (e.g. their Amazon listing URL / ASIN, Shopify, Noon). Then a click on the extension, or a search, matches the store
page to the seller-prepared Drape product and Drape does its job with reliable assets. Collecting sellers' products becomes the core of the
business, so Drape works in every case.
