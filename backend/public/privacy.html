Drape privacy policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026. Applies to the Drape web app, the "Try on with Drape" store button and the Drape Chrome extension.

What Drape does

Drape shows which colour of a garment suits you. You can use a sample person without any photo, or your own face and full-length photos. Drape uses the YouCam API by Perfect Corp. to read your colouring and to show garments, lip colour and earrings on your photo.

The Chrome extension

The extension asks for one permission, activeTab. Only when you click its icon, it reads the address of the page you are on and asks Drape whether a seller has added that product. It never reads or changes the page's content, runs no background code and stores nothing. The address is not saved.

What we never do

  • No accounts, no advertising, no tracking cookies, no selling or sharing of personal data.
  • No use of your photos to train models.
  • No checkout or payment inside Drape: you pay on the store's own website.

Third parties

Photos are processed by the YouCam API (Perfect Corp.). Drape runs on Cloudflare. The stores you buy from have their own privacy policies.

Your choices

Use the sample person to try Drape with no photo at all. Delete your photos at any time with "Delete my photos" in the app; everything else expires after 24 hours.

Questions: use the support link on Drape's Chrome Web Store listing.
